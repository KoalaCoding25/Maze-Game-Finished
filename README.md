A game where you are places in a grid and must reach the end goal before losing all your health. It costs 1 health to move 1 square. enemies are randomly spawned across the board and move in a random pattern whenever you move. there are 4 difficulties staring with 5 enemies on the easiest going up to 2 on the most difficult. colliding with an enemy will take 10 health from you. There are also 2 heath packs around the map that will grant you an extra 10 heath is claimed.
